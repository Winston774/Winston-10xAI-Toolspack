## Final Generation Prompt

Create an explosive, motion-graphics-driven character reveal trailer in 16:9, exactly 13 distinct cuts, 24fps. RIN is a cool, precise adult anime techwear roller-skater with a narrow oval face, sharp violet eyes, pale neutral skin, and a chin-length silver-white razor bob with a clean asymmetric fringe. She wears an oversized matte-black cropped technical shell with a high silver collar, ultraviolet bonded seams, a magenta utility strap across the right shoulder, silver zipper hardware, a fitted white ribbed top, black high-waisted technical shorts with one ultraviolet side panel and a small RX label, black knee guards with silver frames, and chunky black-and-white quad skates with translucent violet wheels. Preserve RIN's exact face, facial proportions, body proportions, skin tone, hairstyle, hair length, hair color, eye shape and color, clothing silhouette, garment construction, materials, accessories, footwear, logos, labels, and color placement in every shot. Never redesign, simplify, recolor, age-shift, substitute, remove, or add any item. No costume changes and no alternate hairstyle.

This film is 80% bold graphic design in motion and 20% character action: giant kinetic RX typography, hard-edged hexagons, split screens, ultraviolet flat fields, scanlines, trajectory rails, barcode strips, UI ticks, halftone, speed streaks and shutter flashes. Palette: ultraviolet, magenta, white, silver, black. Style: premium AAA motion-graphics title sequence x futuristic streetwear skate campaign. Every graphic element moves fast, lands hard and snaps exactly on the beat.

CUT 01 | 0.00-1.00s — Pure graphics on white: a massive ultraviolet hexagon slams into frame, two black scan bars slice across it, silver UI ticks and an RX barcode pulse at the edges; R and X punch in sequentially with a sharp impact quake, and the center of the hexagon opens into a dark aperture.

CUT 02 | 1.00-2.00s — The aperture becomes a hexagonal visor around an extreme close-up of RIN's violet eye; she tracks a moving scan dot, a magenta RGB split flashes, and the visor fractures into flat polygon shards that shoot screen right.

CUT 03 | 2.00-3.10s — On black, enormous white RX letters fill the frame; RIN skates in from frame left, drops into a deep powerslide along the baseline, and her rear wheel carves ultraviolet light through the X while silver fragments kick up like sparks; whip-pan with the shard direction.

CUT 04 | 3.10-4.00s — A three-panel ultraviolet, white and magenta split screen decomposes one aerial skate move into launch, compact spin and wheel-first landing; her silhouette appears as clean stroboscopic phases while huge vertical text ZERO DRAG scrolls behind, then the panels fold into a rotating hexagonal ring.

CUT 05 | 4.00-5.10s — RIN launches through the rotating ring of typography reading SILENT VELOCITY; the camera tracks her slow-motion corkscrew as the words break into flat silver rails, then snap-zooms onto her calm face as one violet wheel crosses the lens.

CUT 06 | 5.10-6.00s — Hard cut to a white editorial card with a huge black GLIDE crossed by an ultraviolet slash; RIN plants one skate on the G and vaults across the word, compressing the letters like a spring-loaded platform before they rebound into a diagonal trajectory rail.

CUT 07 | 6.00-7.00s — On an ultraviolet field, she rolls up the black diagonal rail and performs a backward aerial in three ghost frames tinted magenta, silver and white; giant outlined text SPIN rotates 180 degrees exactly with her body, ending as four stacked scanlines.

CUT 08 | 7.00-8.00s — Kinetic type barrage: SHARP / LIGHT / SILENT / VIOLET slams in one word per beat with shutter-white flashes and tight camera shake; RIN carves a knee-low arc across the foreground while her jacket flares and translucent violet wheel dust bursts into geometric pixels.

CUT 09 | 8.00-9.00s — A black field reveals a giant white wireframe skate bowl tilting in 3D; RIN rides up the vertical grid, kicks free and freezes in a stretched mid-air pose; an ultraviolet hexagon stamps around her as a target lock with silver brackets, scan values and data ticks.

CUT 10 | 9.00-10.10s — The lock releases in one percussive burst; RIN dives toward camera through layered ultraviolet, magenta, white and silver panes that shatter like graphic shutters, each pane exposing a larger fragment of R-X, and her front skate wipes the frame to black.

CUT 11 | 10.10-11.10s — Four full-screen campaign posters snap past: RIN in aerial spin, powerslide, low landing and hands-in-pockets poses; each poster uses a distinct hard-edged layout with oversized numbers 01-04, RX barcodes, hexagonal crops, diagonal slashes and compact scan metadata.

CUT 12 | 11.10-13.00s — On a clean white cyclorama, RIN lands a final double rotation dead center in controlled slow motion, rolls half a meter, then locks into a relaxed hands-in-pockets stance; concentric ultraviolet hexagons, magenta wind rails and shattered typography blast outward, followed by an iconic freeze on her faint confident smile and a hard overexposure to white.

CUT 13 | 13.00-15.00s — Final identity card: enormous black RX dominates a pale field with translucent ultraviolet hexagons, technical arcs, silver scanlines, halftone and a rough black emblem containing a ghosted wheel-bearing/barcode motif; RIN stands relaxed across the letters as her shell ripples in the wind, one last magenta-to-ultraviolet pulse travels through RX, and a crystalline wheel-ring flash punctuates the end.
