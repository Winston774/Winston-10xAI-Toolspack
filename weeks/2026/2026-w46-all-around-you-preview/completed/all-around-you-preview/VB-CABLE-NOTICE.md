# VB-CABLE

The original, unmodified VB-CABLE standard package is provided alongside this prototype.

VB-CABLE is developed and owned by VB-Audio Software / Vincent Burel.

Origin: https://vb-cable.com/ and https://vb-audio.com/Cable/

VB-CABLE is donationware. All participations are welcome. You can support the author or purchase a license at https://shop.vb-audio.com/.

The prototype is tested with `VBCABLE_Driver_Pack46.zip`, downloaded from the official vendor on 2026-11-11.

SHA-256: `bc44e8974191a5a2427d6c979e2dab08dbb09925f3f435dfa2f0ba3e45b6eb90`

The vendor's current distribution conditions permit the original package to accompany another application, including silent deployment, while preserving identification of VB-CABLE and its donationware model. Professional distribution has additional license requirements. See https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm under "VB-CABLE Distribution with another product".

The driver keeps its original signatures and binaries. The Windows endpoint display label is a local user configuration. The driver is independent of EasySurround's MIT license. The original package contains its own readme and end-user terms.

After installation, the vendor recommends restarting Windows. This prototype never restarts Windows automatically and does not disable driver signature enforcement.
